Group your data

Imagine you run a small bookstore. Every row in your sales table describes one book sold, its category, and its price. To find which category earns the most, group the rows by category and add their sales.

Think in groups

Grouping lets you ask one question about many rows at once. The original data stays the same.

sales.groupby("category")["revenue"].sum()

The expression above creates one total for each category. Keep code in its original language so you can run it in your editor.

Why use groupby?

It splits your data into groups, applies a calculation to each group, and combines the results.
